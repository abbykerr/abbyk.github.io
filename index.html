Undergraduate Research

  • Planetary Systems: Exoplanet Atmospheres

    Advisor: Dr. Caroline Morley

    I model cloud particle trajectories across 4 hot Jupiter atmospheres using a global circulation model. After computing settling velocities and simulating particle trajectories, I find that jets carry particles faster than they settle, challenging previous interpretations of hot Jupiter cloud observations.

    Check out my poster: "Atmospheric Particle Behavior within Hot Jupiter Exoplanets"

  • Galaxy Evolution Vertically Integrated Project (GEVIP)

    Advisor: Dr. Steven Finkelstein

    Using dual-epoch JWST observations, I search for supernovae (SNe) via difference imaging. After masking artifacts and calibrating detection thresholds, my search has yielded 7 new transient candidates, expanding the field’s known sample size.

    Check out my poster: "The Hunt for Transients: Supernovae Identification and Classification using JWST "

  • Freshman Research Initiative (FRI): White Dwarf Stars

    Advisor: Dr. Mike Montgomery

    As a freshman in FRI, I visually inspected white dwarf (WD) star spectra from McDonald observatory, discovering > 400 polluted WDs overlooked by machine learning algorithms.

Inspiration

I am immensely inspired to pursue a career path in planetary science due to time spent exploring and observing our intricate planet Earth! Encountering awe from the outdoors and through lived experiences is what fuels my curiosity, as well as recognizing how Earth's geological, astronomical, and biological systems are interconnected. This interdisciplinary lens is what draws me towards the study of exoplanets and planetary science. I want to study how how planets form, evolve, and learn about the conditions that could allow for life to emerge elsewhere in the universe.

Student Mentor

WINS is a community for students within UT's College of Natural Sciences to support young women within their STEM undergraduate career. As a mentor, I lead weekly small group sessions and serve as teaching assistant for WINS seminar, volunteer/host STEM events on campus and in the Austin community, and plan and assist with WINS projects, socials, and events.
